01 / About

I help teams ship releases they can trust.

I'm an ISTQB-certified QA Engineer with 6+ years across mission-critical, financial and high-traffic media products. I've tested a nuclear plant management platform, a retirement platform serving millions of users, and today the BILD and SPORT BILD apps at Axel Springer in Berlin.

My focus is automation that teams can rely on: Cypress, Playwright, Selenium and Appium suites wired into CI/CD, API testing with Postman and Charles Proxy, and SQL-based data validation for ETL pipelines. I've also led QA workstreams, written test strategies, and mentored and onboarded junior engineers.

Alongside work, I'm completing a Master's in Artificial Intelligence at BTU Cottbus–Senftenberg (expected 2027), which shapes how I approach risk-based testing and data quality.
